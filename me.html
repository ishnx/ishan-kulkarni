Intellectual Output

Before the wave.

Publication · Springer · Oct 2019
Exploring Physics Informed Neural Networks for Compressible Flow Prediction
16th Asian Congress of Fluid Mechanics · Temasek Labs, NUS · Singapore MoD
One of Asia's earliest PINN papers — published in 2019. Physical governing equations embedded into neural net loss functions for high-fidelity aerodynamic simulation. 400× speedup over CFD. 93% accuracy on under 2% of training data. The insight — constraints as the most powerful prior a model can have — has stayed with every AI system built since.

Patent · India · IN201941026443
Electromagnetic System Using Gravitational Potential to Condense Atmospheric Water
IIT Madras Design Department · Open Patent · Filed 2019
Atmospheric moisture harvesting for 10M+ households in remote arid India with no electricity access. Uses electromagnetic fields and gravitational potential to drive condensation — no grid, no consumables, no maintenance. Filed from undergraduate research because the problem was too important to leave in a thesis. The same first-principles instinct that drives every product decision.
